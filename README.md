<sub>Subtitles for AI thinking: translate reasoning, task cards and answers into eight languages, with local Ollama and custom providers. Originals stay in the model context.</sub>

**“画一只骑自行车的鹈鹕。”然后看看 AI 给出了什么。** 收集不同模型、不同时间的代码生成作品，把原始来源、成功和翻车一起留下；可以看作品墙，也可以沿时间线逛。<br>
<sub>One prompt, a whole flock. A field guide to documented, code-generated pelicans across models and dates, with original sources, successes and failures kept together.</sub>
